Features

Built for live conversations

Capture, understand, and assist in the moment — without a bot joining the call.

🎙️

Live audio capture

Captures microphone audio and, on Linux, can automatically expose a virtual system-audio source for meeting or media playback.

⚡

Gemini Live transcription

Audio is streamed through the Flask backend to Gemini Live for rolling interim and final transcript events.

💡

Short AI suggestions

The rolling transcript can trigger concise AI suggestions such as clarifying questions, talking points, and next steps.

How it works

From audio to useful context

The browser-style UI and desktop overlay share the same backend, while Electron provides the full desktop experience.

Capture

Hinter captures microphone audio and, where supported, system audio from the desktop.

Transcribe

PCM audio is streamed to the Flask + Socket.IO backend, which maintains a Gemini Live transcription session.

Assist

Final transcript lines are stored in SQLite and can feed the suggestion engine for short, rolling responses.

Positioning

Transparent by design

Hinter is designed as a portfolio project you can explain clearly: what it captures, where transcription happens, and how suggestions are produced.

Hinter

  • Openly disclosed meeting assist
  • No bot needs to join the participant list
  • Clear audio and transcription pipeline
  • Simple UX: listen, transcript, assist

Hidden meeting AI

  • Designed to stay invisible
  • Unclear capture and processing boundaries
  • Harder to explain in a public portfolio
  • Less transparent to meeting participants
